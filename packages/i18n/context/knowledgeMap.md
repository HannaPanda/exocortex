The knowledge map: a full-screen 3D view of every page of a workspace as a glowing point, with lines for references, similar content, entity mentions and the page tree, and the AI chat beside it. While an AI run or an external agent works, the points it reads or writes light up live. Strings are short overlay labels on a dark canvas (legend, a timeline that replays how the workspace grew, a search box that flies the camera to results, a live activity feed); "Wissenskarte" is the screen's name, "Entität" a person, place or project the pages mention.
