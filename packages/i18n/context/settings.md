The account's personal settings pages: which language the interface speaks, notifications, connected apps and API tokens. Headings are short nouns; the paragraphs under them explain a choice in one or two plain sentences.

The same namespace carries the runtime settings forms: the deployment-wide form in the admin area and the per-workspace override form, which share one row per setting (`keys.<group>.<name>.label` and `.help`, grouped under `groups`), the unsaved-changes dialog and the save bar. A label is a short noun phrase, often with its unit in parentheses ("Zeitlimit pro Modellantwort (ms)"); the help text is written for the administrator who has to decide, explains what the switch costs or risks in full sentences, and keeps setting names, model slugs, hosts, time zones and product names (Docling, OpenRouter, Pandoc, TeX Live, MCP) exactly as they are. The workspace settings screen (`workspace`) adds its tabs, the name and slug, the agent memory switch, the member table with its roles, and the workspace's own AI key (`credentials`), whose texts say plainly who pays and what cannot be undone. Everything addresses the reader with "du".
