The application frame around every page: the top bar with search, capture and the panel toggles, the account menu with its entries (help, chats, entities, shares, memory, settings, administration), the workspace switcher, the page tree with its row menus, the page's own top bar and action menu, the context panel's tabs, the connection status and the workspace overview (recently edited pages, loose ends, counts). Entries are short labels a person scans, not sentences; "Strg" is the Ctrl key, and the `*Keywords` messages are space-separated lowercase search words for the command palette, not a sentence.
