The built-in AI assistant: the chat panel beside a page (conversation switcher, model and thinking-level pickers, context meter, the chips above the composer that say what goes to the model, the composer with its slash-command hints, and the live line that reports a running answer and its tool calls), the `/chats` page for finding, renaming, archiving, deleting and saving conversations as pages, the source picker for pinning pages, database views and saved searches, and the read-only list of rule pages in the workspace settings. Buttons, chips and menu items are short; "Werkzeug" is a tool the AI calls and tool names such as `exo_page_read`, model slugs and `/help` stay as they are. Hints, empty states, error messages and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du", and a deletion says plainly that it cannot be undone. The run status lines end in "…" while something is still happening, and "Z." abbreviates characters (Zeichen) in a chip that has little room.
