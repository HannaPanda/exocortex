Sharing a page with people outside its workspace: the share dialog of a page, the account's list of what it shared and what was shared with it, a workspace's overview of all its grants, the inline confirmation before a grant is withdrawn, and the read-only page an anonymous visitor sees behind a public link. Labels, table headings and states are short; explanations and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du" and say plainly who can reach what, and what cannot be undone. The public page is read by somebody without an account who followed a link, so it stays neutral and brief.
