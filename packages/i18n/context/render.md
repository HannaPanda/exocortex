Publishing a page as a PDF: the dialog on a page where a person picks a render template, fills in its variables, starts the build, watches its status and log, and reopens earlier PDFs, plus the workspace page where admins write the templates. Status labels are short and lowercase in a badge; hints are full sentences addressed to the person with "du". Pandoc, LaTeX, Eisvogel (the name of the built-in template), template syntax such as `$title$` and setting keys such as `render.enabled` stay as they are.
