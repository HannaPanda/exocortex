The calendar view of a database: the toolbar with today, the arrows, the date picker and the switch between list, day, week, month and year, the grids and the agenda with their times, the "more entries" line of a crowded day, and the empty state that asks for a date property first. Labels and navigation are short nouns ("Heute", "Nächste Woche", "Ganztags"); range labels join two already formatted dates or times with a plain word ("bis"), never with a dash, and the few full sentences address the person with "du".
