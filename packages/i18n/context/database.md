Notion-style databases, on their own page and embedded as a block inside another page: the view tabs (table, board, gallery), the filter and sort bar, the table with its column menus, the cells people edit in place, the form for relation, rollup and formula columns, and the sheet that shows one row stacked. Labels, menu items, property types and filter operators are short; filter operators and checkbox values complete a sentence in a filter chip ("Status ist nicht leer", "Erledigt angehakt") and stay lowercase there. Empty states, hints and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du", and a confirmation says plainly what is lost; the formula help keeps function names, `prop(...)` and operators as they are, since they are the formula language itself.
