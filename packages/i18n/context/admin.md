The administration area of one eXocortex installation, reachable only by administrators: the overview of figures, the AI model registry with its catalogue and provider list, the AI usage report with costs, tokens and error codes, the user and invitation list, and the list of agent sessions with the way to revert what an agent wrote. Its readers run the deployment, so technical terms (slug, token, alias, median, MCP, OpenRouter) stay as they are, and labels, column headers and figures are short. Explanations and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du", and a confirmation says plainly what is lost or kept.
