The mails this deployment sends, each read by one person in their inbox: address verification, password reset, an invitation to someone who has no account yet, a page shared with or withdrawn from somebody, a digest of new comments, a page an automation mails to its owner, and the notice that an automation failed or switched itself off. Subjects are short and start with "eXocortex: ", bodies are plain sentences addressed with "du" (a greeting line ends with a comma, the next paragraph starts lowercase in German), and names, page titles and rule names in quotation marks are inserted as written.
