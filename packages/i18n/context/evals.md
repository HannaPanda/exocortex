Agent evals: saved tasks for the built-in AI with checks of what must be true afterwards, replayed against several models, reasoning levels or providers and compared side by side. "Eval" stays as the technical term; a "Fall" is one saved task, a "Lauf" one replay of cases, a "Versuch" one attempt (one run of the AI), "Invariante" a check whose failure fails the attempt, "Kriterium" a check of success, "Sandbox" the copied pages a fixture attempt may write in.
