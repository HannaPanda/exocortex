General dialogs, sheets and panels of the application frame: the trash with its permanent deletion, quick capture, the web clipper and share target, moving a page to another workspace, suggesting where a page belongs, creating a page from a template, importing Markdown, and a page's activity history with restoring an earlier version. Titles and buttons are short labels; descriptions and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du", and a confirmation spells out exactly what cannot be undone.
