Inviting somebody to this deployment, from the administration's user list and from a workspace's settings: the invite dialog (address, workspace, role, admin rights, the link shown once afterwards) and the table of sent invitations with their status, resend and withdraw actions and the confirmation before withdrawing. Labels, roles, statuses and table headings are short; explanations and confirmations are full sentences addressed to the person with "du" and say plainly what the link does and what cannot be undone.
