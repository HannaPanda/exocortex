The surface of a single page and the context panel beside it: the page header with its symbol picker (names of icons, icon groups and colours), the cover image, the overview of sub-pages, the page properties dialog, the properties, references and comments tabs of the panel, the comparison of two saved versions of a page, and the PDF viewer. Buttons, tabs, badges and icon names are short labels; hints and empty states are full sentences addressed to the person with "du", in a calm and matter-of-fact register. Icon names are also what the symbol search matches, so they are the everyday word for the pictured thing, not a literal translation of the English icon name.
