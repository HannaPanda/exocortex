The entities screen (`/entitaeten`): a directory of the people, hosts, services, projects and other things that keep turning up in a person's pages, with a filterable list, one entity's profile (facts, connections, the pages that mention it), a dialog to create one by hand, a form to link a page by hand, the list of suggested names waiting to be confirmed or dismissed, and the one-time setup that creates the entity database. Labels, entity types and buttons are short; hints and empty states are full sentences addressed to the person with "du". Placeholders such as "fpb2, Flauschipanda 2" are example spellings typed by a person and should read like plausible examples, not be translated word for word.
