The help page (`/hilfe`), which lists what this deployment can do: the page heading and intro, the search field, the "new for you" filter and the button that marks everything as seen, the side navigation of feature areas with their one-sentence descriptions, the "Neu" badge and "seit" date on an entry, and the labels of the box that says where a feature is found (screen, shortcut, agent tools, settings, background). The feature entries themselves are not in this namespace; they are in `features`. Labels are short; the intro, the area descriptions and the empty states are full sentences addressed to the person with "du".
