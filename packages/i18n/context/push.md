Push notifications on a phone's or desktop's lock screen, rendered on the server in the recipient's language: the line announcing a new comment ("{name} hat kommentiert", then page title and a preview) and the calendar reminder (how far away the appointment is, its title, then its time and place). A title is read at a glance and must stay short; `{time}`, `{from}`, `{to}` and `{toDate}` arrive already formatted for the locale, so only the words around them are translated, and "Uhr" is dropped wherever the language does not say it.
