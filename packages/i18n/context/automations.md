The automations screen of a workspace: the list of rules with their scope, triggers and action, the run log beneath it, and the dialog in which a workspace owner writes a rule (where it watches, what it reacts to or which schedule it follows, and whether it sends a signed webhook, starts an AI run or mails the owner). Trigger, status and origin labels are short, and run statuses are lowercase because they sit in a badge; schedule clauses read as one phrase ("Täglich um 07:00 (Europe/Berlin)"). Hints and warnings are full sentences addressed to the person with "du". Setting keys, HTTP header names, HMAC-SHA256, POST, cron syntax such as `@daily` and IANA zone names stay exactly as written.
