# Additional individual grants

`LICENSE` (PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0) is what applies to you unless you hold
a written grant from the licensor saying otherwise. Its **No Other Rights**
section says so in both directions:

> These terms do not allow you to sublicense or transfer any of your licenses to
> anyone else, or prevent the licensor from granting licenses to anyone else.

So the licensor can hand individual people terms of their own without changing
anything for everybody else. This file records that such grants exist, because a
licence nobody can see is a licence nobody can rely on.

## What is granted

| Granted on | To                       | Scope                                                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2026-09-16 | one named individual (1) | Unrestricted use, including commercial use, with one exception (2) |

1. The holder is identified in a signed document held by the licensor, not here.
   Naming a person in a public repository is a privacy decision, and it was
   taken the other way. If you think a grant applies to you, you have a copy of
   it; if you have no copy, `LICENSE` applies to you.
2. The grant does **not** include the right to make the software available to
   third parties under terms other than `LICENSE`. The holder may use, change,
   run, host and sell products built on eXocortex; the holder may not relicense
   eXocortex itself to the public.
